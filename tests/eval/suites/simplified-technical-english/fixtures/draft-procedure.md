# Coolant pump replacement

The utilization of the aforementioned coolant pump assembly module is prohibited whilst the system is being pressurized by the ground crew, and it's important that you don't attempt it.

1. Ensure that the main isolation valve has been closed by the technician; verify the indicator.
2. Prior to commencing, it is necessary that the operator should facilitate the removal of the primary coolant distribution manifold bracket.
3. The pump is disconnected by the technician, e.g. using the standard tooling.
4. Subsequent to the aforementioned steps, the replacement pump's installation can be effectuated.

The above procedure was developed by engineering and it is being reviewed presently. Approximately 2 liters of coolant will be drained. The system's pressure must not exceed 3 bar. Refer to fig. 4 for the assembly diagram. Additional information is available in the maintenance manual. Contact the supervisor if there are any issues whatsoever.
