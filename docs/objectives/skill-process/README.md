## Research and targets

- [`research/`](research/README.md) holds evidence, rejected candidates, contradictions, and source
  mappings.
- [`targets.md`](targets.md) stays a concise URL queue. It does not carry research documentation.

## Bounds

- No universal house pipeline.
- No standardization on one engine, framework, platform, or game.
- No implementation of supporting skills.
- No page quotas: a weak target may produce zero pages.
- At most six targets and two pages per target in each recursive wave.
- No third recursive wave in this objective.
